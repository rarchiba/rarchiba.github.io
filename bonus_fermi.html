2D Pulse Portraits

Here's a sample of the 2-D pulse profile & some slices across. This allows us to get all the information from each photon. E.g. most of the pulsars are more peaked at higher energy, so a high energy photon can provide much more info than a low energy one.

Is the Signal Real?

If we process the data every year or so, assuming a fixed $\gamma=13/3$ we see the posterior distribution of the strain A evolve. We nicely match the Fermi team's published upper limit at 12.5 years of ~$A < 1 \times 10^{-14}$, and see it tighten as we add more data. This is at least consistent with the signal being real.
